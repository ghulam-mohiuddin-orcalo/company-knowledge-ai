Northwind Analytics IT Security Policy

Passwords and sign-in
All company accounts must use multi-factor authentication. Passwords must be at least 14 characters long and must never be shared or reused across services.

Lost or stolen devices
Report a lost or stolen laptop or phone to the IT Service Desk within one hour by calling extension 4357. The IT Service Desk will remotely lock and wipe the device.

Phishing
Forward suspicious emails to phishing@northwind.example using the Report Phishing button. Never open attachments from unknown senders.

Software installation
Only software from the approved company catalogue may be installed on company laptops. Requests for new software are reviewed by the security team within five working days.

Data classification
Confidential customer data must never be stored on personal devices or personal cloud storage.
