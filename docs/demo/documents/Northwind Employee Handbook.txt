Northwind Analytics Employee Handbook

Working hours
Core working hours are 10:00 to 16:00, Monday to Friday. Outside core hours, employees choose when to work in agreement with their manager.

Annual leave
Full-time employees receive 25 days of annual leave per year, plus public holidays. Part-time employees receive annual leave in proportion to their contracted hours. Up to five unused days of annual leave can be carried over into the next year.

Remote work
Employees may work remotely up to three days per week. Remote work from another country requires written approval from People Operations at least two weeks in advance.

Expenses
Business travel must be booked through the company travel portal. Meal expenses during business travel are reimbursed up to 40 pounds per day with itemised receipts.

Parental leave
Employees with at least six months of service receive 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave.
